Heto for Dentistry

A fresh approach to healthcare education management.

Heto helps institutions prepare future healthcare professionals more efficiently. Dental schools use it to run every part of their student community outreach programs, from scheduling and clinic management to evaluations and flexible reporting, with data managed securely in the cloud.

Scheduling & rotations

Assign students to sites ahead of time against your academic calendar. Students see upcoming rotations on their dashboard.

Clinic management

Manage every partner clinic, assign site administrators, and track preceptors, required documents and submitted case logs.

Case logs

Design case logs to your school's needs. Students log cases at their assigned site, and entries route to preceptors for approval.

Evaluations

Build custom rubrics and templates for any purpose, and let Heto's scheduler trigger each evaluation at the right time.

Approvals

An approval hierarchy with automatic notifications and reminders, so every approver knows what needs attention and when.

Student portfolios

Students showcase case logs alongside academic and extracurricular credentials, a portfolio that complements their LinkedIn profile.

Document management

Track certificates and immunization records, with reminders before anything expires or comes up for renewal.

Reports & dashboards

Ready-made reports common to North American dental schools, plus a builder for your own reports and charts.

Roles & admin portal

Role-based permissions for clear segregation of duties, and an admin portal to configure Heto to your school's requirements.

Verify US businesses and professionals.

Two APIs, one platform, one key. Provero pre-loads and indexes official state records so lookups come back as clean, consistent JSON in milliseconds, with no scraping on demand.

/business/search

Provero Business

Corporations, LLCs and partnerships: registration status, registered agent and filing dates.

/license/search

Provero License

Contractors, healthcare workers, real estate agents and more: license type, number, status and expiry.
